Expression Reference

1. Variables

%tagTags.
%titleTitle 1 or Title 2.
%title1Title 1.
%title2Title 2.
%ratingRating. For example, 100 represents 5 stars, and -1 means no rating.
%completion_statusCompletion status.
%progressProgress, as a percentage. For example, 50 represents 50%.
%durationTotal duration in milliseconds.
%release_dateRelease date, formatted as YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS. Empty when unset; a bare %release_date matches only items that have one.
Comparands may be a full date and time, or a partial date covering a period:
2026 or "2026" - The whole year 2026.
"2026-1" or "2026/1" - January 2026.
"2026-1-2" or "2026/1/2" - January 2, 2026.
Operators apply to that period rather than to the text:
%release_date = 2026 - Within 2026.
%release_date >= 2026 - From the start of 2026.
%release_date > 2026 - After the end of 2026.
%release_date < "2026-1" - Before January 2026.
%release_date <= "2026-1-2" - On or before January 2, 2026.
%release_date in (2026, 2027) - Within 2026 or 2027.
A full date and time, or anything that is not a date, is compared as plain text.

2. Variable Operators

=Equal to. For example: %title = "Here's the title"
>Greater than. For example: %rating > 3
>=Greater than or equal to.
<Less than.
<=Less than or equal to.
... IN (...)Is contained in. For example:
%rating in (3, 4, 5) - Matches items whose ratings are 3, 4, or 5.
%tag.language in (A, B) - Matches items whose "language" tag category contains "A" or "B".
(A, B) in %tag.artists - Matches items whose "artists" tag category contains both "A" and "B".
"apple" in %tag - Matches items that contain the "apple" tag.

3. Logical Operators

ANDLogical AND. For example: %tag.artists = A AND %title = "Here's the title"
ORLogical OR. For example: %title1 = "Here's title 1" OR %title2 = "Here's title 2"
NOTLogical NOT. For example: NOT %tag = "SomeTag"

4. Parentheses

By default, operators are evaluated from left to right. Use (...) to give the enclosed expression higher precedence. For example:

%tag.artists = abc AND (%tag.language = langA OR NOT %tag.lang = langB)

The expression will be evaluated in the following order:

1. NOT %tag.lang = langB ==> Result1

2. %tag.language = langA OR Result1 ==> Result2

3. %tag.artists = abc AND Result2 ==> Result3
